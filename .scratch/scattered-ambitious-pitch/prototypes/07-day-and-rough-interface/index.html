Things I assumed that you have not settled - react to these
  1. The one click puts the note's text into the box you are typing in. Whether it should instead take you to another place is open in ticket 13.
  2. A note appears after 4 typed words when it shares 2 or more words with them. Real retrieval is not defined yet.
  3. The key combinations are placeholders: Ctrl+; capture, Ctrl+Enter new dialog beside, Ctrl+] link the selected phrase.
  4. The split hint cuts at line breaks, or at sentences if there are none. You can join two thoughts by clicking the divider.
  5. Related notes in capture are plain and not clickable: optional and easy to ignore.
