# What they already pay for

## Gist

A scattered ambitious self-payer already spreads money across a wide band: free tiers and low subscriptions ($3–$12/month for Goblin Tools Pro, Focusmate Plus, Routinery, Todoist Pro) through mid-tier daily planners ($17–$34/month for Sunsama and Akiflow) up to premium AI schedulers ($19–$29/seat/month for Motion). One-time task apps like Things 3 cost roughly $10–$35 per Apple platform. First-party sources show referral programs and peer recommendation (Todoist partners, Sunsama credits, Focusmate gift cards, Goblin Tools shareable lists), but published subscription retention or churn rates were not found for these products.

---

## How to read this note

All prices are for **individual self-pay** unless noted. Where a vendor publishes only annual billing on its pricing page, both annual and monthly figures are listed when a primary source gives them. **Retention rate** means a published percentage of subscribers who renew; when none was found, that is stated explicitly.

---

## Price bands at a glance

| Band (USD) | Examples | What the payment buys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free tier | Todoist Beginner, Notion Free, Focusmate (3 sessions/week), Goblin Tools web, Tiimo mobile free, Routinery (2 routines) | Core capture/planning with hard limits |
| ~$3–$8/month | Goblin Tools Pro, Focusmate Plus, Routinery Premium, Todoist Pro (annual equiv.) | Neurodivergent-friendly utilities, body doubling, routines, or task limits lifted |
| ~$8–$15/month | Tiimo Pro (platform-dependent), Todoist Pro (monthly), Notion Plus (annual equiv.) | Full-feature mobile/web planning or workspace upgrades |
| ~$17–$34/month | Sunsama, Akiflow (annual), Motion (annual), Akiflow (monthly) | Opinionated daily planning, time-blocking, AI calendar scheduling |
| One-time | Things 3 (per Apple platform) | Perpetual license per device type; Things Cloud sync free |

## Task managers

### Todoist

**Published prices (Todoist, 2026):** Beginner free; Pro **$7/month** or **$60/year** ($5/month billed yearly); Business **$10/user/month** or **$8/user/month** billed yearly. Pro unlocks calendar layout, task duration, time-blocking, custom reminders, AI features (Ramble, Todoist Assist, Email Assist), and higher project/filter limits. ([Todoist plans FAQ](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/billing/todoist-plans-pricing-and-billing-faq-Vq2z0HWL6))

**Stay / tell a peer:** Todoist reports **50+ million** users, **1+ million Pro users**, and **30+ million** app downloads on its homepage (Todoist, 2026, [todoist.com](https://www.todoist.com/)). It runs a **Partner Program** with unique referral links; partners earn up to **25% commission** when referred users upgrade from Beginner to a paid plan within **90 days** (Todoist, 2026, [Partner Programs](https://www.todoist.com/help/account-and-billing/plans/todoist-partner-programs-t8t2hZ0Z)). **Subscription retention rate:** not found in primary sources.

### Things 3

**Published prices (Cultured Code / Apple App Store, 2026):** One-time purchase per platform, **not a subscription**. Things Cloud sync is free. US App Store list prices: iPhone **$9.99**, iPad **$19.99**, Mac **$34.99** (Cultured Code, 2026, [Getting Things support](https://culturedcode.com/things/support/articles/2803552/); Apple App Store listings for Things 3 on iPhone, iPad, and Mac). Payment buys a GTD-style task manager with reminders, calendar integration, tags, and Apple ecosystem integrations.

**Stay / tell a peer:** Things supports **Apple Family Sharing** so family members can share the app purchase (Cultured Code, 2026, [Getting Things](https://culturedcode.com/things/support/articles/2803552/)). **Retention rate** and **referral program:** not found.

### Notion

**Published prices (Notion, 2024–2026):** Free for individuals organizing personal projects. **Plus: $10/member/month** billed annually or **$12/member/month** billed monthly (Notion, 2024, [Plus plan pricing update](https://www.notion.com/releases/2024-06-26)). **Business: $20/member/month** on the public pricing page (Notion, 2026, [notion.com/pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing)); Notion documents monthly and yearly billing options but the pricing page accessed did not list a separate USD monthly Business figure. Plus adds unlimited file uploads, 30-day page history, and collaboration features; Business adds Notion AI, SSO, and team controls.

**Stay / tell a peer:** Notion offers a **Free Plan** indefinitely for solo use and documents student/education access to Plus (Notion, 2026, [Pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing); [Change your plan](https://www.notion.com/help/upgrade-or-downgrade-your-plan)). **Retention rate** and **consumer referral program:** not found.

## Opinionated calendars and daily planners

### Motion

**Published prices (Motion / Apple App Store, 2026):** **Pro AI $19/seat/month** and **Business AI $29/seat/month** on the annual-billing pricing page (Motion, 2026, [usemotion.com/pricing](https://www.usemotion.com/pricing)). iOS in-app purchases list **$44.99/month** and **$294.99/year** (NexusBird, 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/motion-tasks-ai-scheduling/id1580440623)). No permanent free plan; **7-day trial**. Payment buys AI task planning, auto-scheduling against calendar, projects, docs, and integrations.

**Stay / tell a peer:** Motion claims use by **1M+ busy professionals and teams** (NexusBird, 2026, [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/motion-tasks-ai-scheduling/id1580440623)). Homepage tagline: “Finish 137% more work” (Motion, 2026, [pricing](https://www.usemotion.com/pricing)) — a marketing claim, not a published study. **Retention rate** and **referral program:** not found.

### Sunsama

**Published prices (Sunsama, 2026):** One Pro plan: **$22/user/month** monthly or **$17/user/month** billed yearly (**$204/user/year**). No permanent free tier; **14-day full-feature trial, no credit card required** (Sunsama, 2026, [Pricing](https://www.sunsama.com/pricing); [Billing overview](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/billing/overview/)). Payment buys guided daily planning, calendar/task integrations, focus mode, workload warnings, and AI/MCP/Zapier.

**Stay / tell a peer:** Sunsama’s pricing manifesto states the company became **profitable in 2022** and updated prices in **2026 after five years** at the prior rate (Sunsama, 2026, [Pricing Manifesto](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/billing/pricing-manifesto/)). **Referral program:** trial signups via your link receive **$22 account credit**; when they upgrade, you receive **$22 credit** on your next charge (Sunsama, 2026, [Billing overview](https://help.sunsama.com/docs/billing/overview/)). Public pricing page embeds user testimonials including multi-year use (“2 years and counting”) (Sunsama, 2026, [Pricing](https://www.sunsama.com/pricing)). **Subscription retention rate:** not found.

### Akiflow

**Published prices (Akiflow, 2026):** No free plan; **7-day trial** (card required). **Pro $34/month** billed monthly or **$19/month** billed yearly (**$228/year**). Longer **Believer** options: **$357.60 for two years** ($14.90/month) or **$699 for five years** (Akiflow, 2026, [akiflow.com/pricing](https://akiflow.com/pricing)). Optional **AI Meeting Assistant add-on $19/month**. Payment buys unified calendar + task inbox, time-blocking, integrations, Aki AI assistant, and MCP connector.

**Stay / tell a peer:** Akiflow publishes customer testimonials on its pricing page; no user-count or retention statistics. Team plans offer **40% discount** for invited members (Akiflow, 2026, [Onboarding your Team](https://product.akiflow.com/help/articles/3951313-onboarding-your-team)). **Retention rate** and **referral program:** not found.

## Focus tools and attention support

### Goblin Tools

**Published prices (Goblin Tools / Skyhook BV, 2026):** Core web tools **free, ad-free, forever** (Goblin Tools, 2026, [About](https://ios.goblin.tools/About)). **Goblin Tools Pro: $3 / €3 / £3 per month** on the website with a **1-week free trial** (Goblin Tools, 2026, [pro/join](https://goblin.tools/pro/join); [Terms of Service](https://www.goblin.tools/Terms)). iOS in-app purchases: **$3.99/month** or **$39.99/year** (Goblin Tools, 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/goblin-tools/id6449003064)). Pro adds cross-device sync, per-tool history, templates, custom Formalizer tones, and **shareable lists** peers can open and adapt.

**Stay / tell a peer:** Pro includes **“Hand off a plan — send someone a list they can open and adapt as their own”** (Goblin Tools, 2026, [Pro about](https://goblin.tools/pro/about)). **Retention rate:** not found.

### Tiimo

**Published prices (Tiimo, 2026):** **Free tier** on iOS/Android (basic planner; web app requires Pro). Pro is monthly or yearly; **Tiimo’s FAQ does not publish fixed USD amounts** — “your local price will be shown in the App Store / Google Play / web (Paddle) at checkout” (Tiimo, 2026, [FAQ / pricing](https://www.tiimoapp.com/faq)). US App Store in-app purchase options include multiple Pro price points (e.g. **$11.99**, **$7.00**, **$12.00**, **$14.00**, **$36.00**, **$42.00**, **$48.00**, **$54.00** tiers listed) (Tiimo, 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328)). **7-day trial** applies to yearly subscription only. Payment buys visual daily planning, focus timer, AI co-planner, calendar sync, and cross-platform access.

**Stay / tell a peer:** App Store listing includes user quotes recommending the app to others with ADHD; Tiimo markets **family sharing** (up to 5 profiles) and **100% ad-free** positioning (Tiimo, 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328); [Why Tiimo isn’t fully free](https://www.tiimoapp.com/resource-hub/why-tiimo-went-freemium)). **Retention rate** and **referral program:** not found.

### Routinery

**Published prices (Routinery, 2026):** **Free: 2 routines**, unlimited checklists. Premium: **$5.00/month** or **$36.00/year** (Routinery, 2026, [habit-tracking alternatives blog](https://www.routinery.app/blog/habit-tracking-app-alternatives) — official Routinery publication citing App Store/Google Play listings, checked 2026-08-19). US App Store confirms **$5.00/month** and **$36.00/year** in-app purchases (Routinery, 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/routine-planner-habit-tracker/id1450486923)). Payment buys voice-guided routine timers, habit tracking, and calendar integration.

**Stay / tell a peer:** Routinery editorial content states the product is used by **more than 5 million people** (Routinery, 2026, [blog footer](https://www.routinery.app/blog/habit-tracking-app-alternatives)). **Guest Pass** offers **30-day free Pro access** via routinery.app/guest-pass (Routinery, 2026, [Guest Pass](https://www.routinery.app/guest-pass)). Homepage user quotes describe long-term routine dependence (Routinery, 2026, [routinery.app](https://www.routinery.app/)). **Retention rate:** not found.

## Body doubling

### Focusmate

**Published prices (Focusmate, 2026):** **Free: 3 sessions/week**, no credit card. **Plus: $8/month** billed annually or **$12/month** billed monthly for unlimited sessions (Focusmate, 2026, [Pricing](https://www.focusmate.com/pricing/); [FAQ](https://www.focusmate.com/faq/)). **Gift cards** available (Focusmate, 2026, [Pricing](https://www.focusmate.com/pricing/)). Business tiers from **$50/month** (team) and **$750/month + setup** (enterprise) — employer-facing, not self-pay (Focusmate, 2026, [Business](https://www.focusmate.com/business/)).

**Stay / tell a peer:** Homepage reports **12 million+** completed sessions and members from **150+ countries** (Focusmate, 2026, [focusmate.com](https://www.focusmate.com/)). February 2024 survey of **834 active members** (5+ sessions in the prior week): **99%** reported productivity improvement; **96%** said it helps follow through on intentions; **100%** of members with diagnosed ADHD reported productivity improvement (Focusmate, 2024, [Virtual coworking research](https://www.focusmate.com/virtual-coworking-research/)). Testimonials page shows users with **hundreds to thousands of sessions** (Focusmate, 2026, [Testimonials](https://www.focusmate.com/testimonials/)). **Subscription retention/churn rate:** not found. 2019 strategic plan discusses improving retention but does not publish a target or actual rate (Focusmate, 2019, [2019 strategic plan](https://www.focusmate.com/blog/2019-strategic-plan/)).

## Cross-cutting findings

### What self-pay buys, by category

| Category | Typical payment | Primary job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task manager | $0–$7/mo or one-time $10–$35 | Capture, sort, and schedule tasks |
| Workspace / second brain | $0–$12/mo | Notes, databases, personal OS |
| Daily planner / time-blocker | $17–$34/mo | Ritualized planning against calendar |
| AI auto-scheduler | $19–$44.99/mo | Algorithmic day planning |
| Neurodivergent utilities | $0–$8/mo | Task breakdown, routines, visual planning |
| Body doubling | $0–$12/mo | Accountability via live coworking sessions |

### Evidence that people stay or tell a peer

| Product | First-party “stay” signal | First-party “tell a peer” signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Todoist | 1M+ Pro users; 19+ years in market | Partner referral program (25% commission, 90-day cookie) |
| Sunsama | Profitable since 2022; multi-year user quotes | $22 referral credit both ways |
| Focusmate | 12M+ sessions; power users with 1,000–6,500+ sessions | Gift cards; neuropsychologist quote recommending to ADHD patients (research page) |
| Goblin Tools | Core tools stay free | Shareable Pro lists |
| Routinery | 5M+ users (company blog) | Guest Pass for 30-day Pro trial |
| Motion | 1M+ users (App Store) | Not found |
| Akiflow | Testimonials only | Not found |
| Tiimo | Ad-free freemium; family profiles | App Store testimonials |
| Things 3 | Family Sharing | Not found |
| Notion | Free solo tier; education discounts | Not found |

### What was not found

Across all products checked, **no primary source published a subscription retention rate, monthly churn percentage, or renewal-rate benchmark** for individual self-pay plans. User counts, session counts, survey self-reports, and referral-program mechanics are available; hard retention metrics are not.
