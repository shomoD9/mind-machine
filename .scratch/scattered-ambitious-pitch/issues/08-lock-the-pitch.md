## Question

Assemble the pitch from the locked decisions, including [Last pass on passive cues](13-last-pass-on-passive-cues.md), and confirm it with the human.

The pitch states the true problem, who it is for, what the product does, the day and the rough interface, and why the scattered ambitious person pays, keeps paying, and tells a peer. This ticket ends when the human locks that pitch. The agent does not lock it alone.

Also confirm the [MVP scope](../mvp.md) alongside the product vision. The MVP can be very ambitious, but each included behavior must have a concrete, settled product decision. Check that its capture-to-action journey is complete and that no included feature silently depends on an unresolved choice. Engineering difficulty is not a reason to shrink it. Resolve required choices before locking; do not present an incomplete collection of commitments as a finished MVP.
