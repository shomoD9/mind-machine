# Form of the creation session

Map: [Scattered ambitious pitch](../map.md)
Type: grilling
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

A maker stays because the dump and the creation session are one product. [When an intention comes back](09-when-an-intention-comes-back.md) says the dumped intention governs what they make only when a cue retrieves it at that moment. A separate scroll they must remember to open can remain notes they never act on.

The human wants the simplest possible daily habit, borrowed from addictive consumer behavior. The candidates already named are an infinite scroll of their own thoughts, short-form pieces of those thoughts, and an injection into a feed they already open.

Which of those, or what other surface, is the creation session? Which are only ways of spreading the product? The price band waits on this answer. This is a conversation. The human chooses the form. The agent does not.

## Answer

The form is a point of decision. The word "creation session" is retired.

The point of thinking is the dump. A thought arrives, the maker puts it in, and does not organize it.

A dumped note then appears inside an app they already have open. There is no home they have to remember to open. A scroll of their own notes that they must open is not the product.

A point of decision is one click. They choose where that note goes, and the app takes them there: a post, a Substack draft, a chat, and others. The piece is made after they arrive. The point of decision is the act. A note they do not take anywhere has not worked. How full the draft is depends on which point of decision, and that stays unlocked.

The list of apps and points of decision is [Last pass on passive cues](13-last-pass-on-passive-cues.md).

Once in a while the app asks them to confirm a suggested label on a note it could not place. That is a correction, not a point of decision and not a home. Points of decision do not wait on it.
