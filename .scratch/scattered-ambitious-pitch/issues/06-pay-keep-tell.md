## Question

Given [What they already pay for](03-what-they-pay-for.md) and the spine from [Name the spine](04-name-the-spine.md), what is the business in the pitch?

The answer has to say what the scattered ambitious person pays for, why they keep paying, and why they tell a peer. Venture-scale versus a smaller company is not this ticket. The buyer is the person. This is a conversation. The human locks the business. The agent does not invent it.

## Answer

The first buyer is the maker: a scattered ambitious person whose work is to ideate and then create or publish. They pay for one product in which the dump and the creation session are the same thing. They keep paying because new thoughts have nowhere else to go, and a creation session starts from those thoughts. An exportable archive is something they can leave.

They tell another maker that they dump without organizing, and that when they sit down to make something the work starts from that dump. They also tell from pride, the way people show a tool they love, including people who talk about tools in public. The marketed life is passive productivity: calm, laid back, and extremely creative. The passivity is the upkeep. The making is the act.

The price band is not locked. It waits until the form of the creation session is chosen. Venture-scale versus a smaller company stays unresolved.
