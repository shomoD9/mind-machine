## Question

[When an intention comes back](09-when-an-intention-comes-back.md) says an intention governs the next act only when a cue retrieves that act at the moment of action: a focal event, a monitored time, an if-then link, or an external trigger the person actually consults. Related notes during capture, and a later review, can leave them with notes they never act on.

Given the directional capture sketch in [One product or a series](05-one-product-or-a-series.md) — parallel bursts, typing or speech, the person does not classify — what must a capture preserve so that cue exists at the moment of action?

Which parts of the sketch are required for that cue, and which stay optional: adjacent threads, speech, similar notes beside the cursor, the blurred screen, Explode, wikilinks?

This is a conversation. The human decides. The agent does not lock the chrome for them.

## Comments

### 2026-09-29 — GPT-6 — Codex App

Claimed the next frontier ticket. Asked the human whether capture preserves exact words and separate thoughts or permits extraction/rewriting, and whether it retains a source reference or surrounding content. Recommended exact words with revisable inferred connections, plus a source reference when available. These are proposals awaiting the human's answers. Capture interactions remain to be discussed after this round.

The human accepted preserving separate thoughts and their wording, with the qualification that very minor corrections may make the writing legible. Inferred connections remain revisable. They also accepted retaining a source or draft reference when available, without classification. This settles the first round; the ticket remains claimed while capture interactions are discussed.

The human confirmed that adjacent writing spaces are required: a new thought can be opened beside an unfinished one. Related notes during capture are optional and easy to ignore. The remaining capture interactions are still open.

The human confirmed a simple capture overlay that blurs and darkens the background, with a shortcut to open and dismiss it. Explode means using a hotkey to split a massive captured block into constituent atomic notes by meaning; it belongs to the product vision and is explicitly outside the MVP. Wikilinks are a first-class product interaction: select a word or phrase in the current dialog and use a simple hotkey to create a new dialog linked from that selection. Exact key combinations remain for the rough interface.

Two boundaries need clarification before resolution: whether a multi-thought block remains intact in the MVP with Explode excluded, and whether the linked dialog is an independent note or a subordinate fragment. No answer to either has been assumed.

The human answered both boundaries. A linked dialog is an independent note, retrievable and actionable by itself, with the selected phrase kept as its source reference. A multi-thought block gets a split hint, and the human wants the hint to be able to run Explode. They asked for a product case for Explode at capture, since the backend already chunks captured text by idea space when it enters the wiki or knowledge graph. That case is unresolved, and so is whether Explode enters the MVP. Explode remains listed as beyond the MVP until it is settled.

The human accepted that a point-of-decision card shows a backend chunk, and that the split hint shows the maker the proposed boundary so they can see and fix it. Accepting the hint is Explode. Speech is outside the MVP: the product does not convert speech to text, and the maker can dictate into it with their own tool, such as Wispr. Native speech capture is a later addition, worth building only if the point of decision follows the maker's cursor. That cursor idea is a direction for [Last pass on passive cues](13-last-pass-on-passive-cues.md) and is not settled.

The human chose to admit Explode to the MVP only as accepting or adjusting the split hint. The standalone hotkey that splits a massive block on demand stays beyond the MVP. They confirmed the cursor idea is an open direction.
