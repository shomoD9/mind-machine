## Question

Why do the tools a scattered ambitious person already reaches for fail to hold them?

Cover task lists, second brains, calendars, focus timers, body doubling, and capture inboxes. Name specific products only when a primary source supports the claim: the product's own documentation, a paper on abandonment or personal information management, or a first-party account of how the product is meant to be used.

Read `CONTEXT.md` at the repo root. The person is ambitious, multi-project, and loses intentions they already formed. The question is why these tools lose that person, not which app is best.

Separate evidence from inference. Where abandonment is folklore rather than a sourced finding, say so. Do not propose a product.

## Answer

Productivity tools fail to hold a scattered ambitious person when the cost of keeping the system current—visibility, integration, processing, and honest upkeep—outruns the benefit, especially as project load scales. The research treats this as a maintenance-and-fit problem more than a motivation problem.
