## Question

[Form of the creation session](11-form-of-the-creation-session.md) locks where a dumped intention meets the maker. This ticket runs after that, after capture, after the price band, and after the rough day. It is the last pass before [Lock the pitch](08-lock-the-pitch.md).

The maker already has habits that take no effort: scrolling a feed, opening a chat, sitting in an editor. The pitch wants a dumped intention to show up inside those habits, with almost no effort, the way a feed shows the next item.

Find other places a dumped note can appear, and other places one click can take it, beyond a modal beside an editor, a hotkey overlay on a chat, and a card injected into a feed. The dump is the point of thinking. A point of decision is the click: the maker chooses where the note goes, and the app takes them there. The note appears inside an app they already have open. There is no home.

For each one, say whether the cue can change the next act, or whether it only produces a feeling of inspiration. Use primary sources on cues at the moment of action. [When an intention comes back](09-when-an-intention-comes-back.md) is the starting point. Do not propose a build. The human reads this before the pitch is locked.
