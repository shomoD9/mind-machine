## Question

For a scattered ambitious person — serious ambition, many open projects, heavy context switching, and a weak hold on intentions they already formed — which failure is load-bearing?

The candidate failures are:

- losing hold of intentions already formed
- the pain of organizing thoughts
- context switching itself
- holding many projects at once
- the inability to stay on one thing

Read `CONTEXT.md` at the repo root before starting. A clinical diagnosis is not required for this person. Hyperactivity and attention deficit are texture, not the research question.

Use primary sources: peer-reviewed work and the originating texts of the constructs (prospective memory, working memory, executive function, task switching, goal neglect). Follow each claim to the source that owns it. Secondary summaries are not evidence.

Report which failures the sources treat as causes and which they treat as consequences. If the sources do not crown a single winner, say so and show the disagreement. Do not propose a product.

## Answer

Sources do not crown one of the five candidate failures as the single load-bearing cause. The closest upstream failure is **losing active control by formed intentions and goals** under concurrent load — goal neglect and prospective-memory monitoring/planning, not mere recall. Holding many projects and context switching are amplifiers and costs; organizing thoughts is compensatory; inability to stay on one thing is downstream.
