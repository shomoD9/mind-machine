## Question

Given the spine named in [Name the spine](04-name-the-spine.md), is this pitch one product or a series of tools?

Say what the pitch includes, and what would be a later product that this pitch does not describe. This is a conversation with the human. The agent does not pick the cut for them.

## Answer

One product. It has two moments, and the second is what makes the first matter.

Capture keeps fidelity with parallel bursts: typing or speech, many threads, the person does not classify. The hotkeys, the blurred screen, Explode, and wikilinks stay a directional sketch, not a locked behavior.

Retrieval puts an already-formed intention back in control of the next act. Without that, the person has not acted. A similar note beside the cursor, or a later browse, only counts when it changes what they do. A capture app now and a retrieval app later is the series this pitch does not describe.

Whether captures follow the person across surfaces is still open.
