# Scattered ambitious pitch

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A locked product-and-business pitch for a self-pay product used by the scattered ambitious person: the problem that is actually true, what the product does, a day in the life plus a rough interface, and a business in which that person pays, keeps paying, and tells a peer.

Record both the product vision and an ambitious MVP whose product decisions are fully concrete and explicitly settled by the human.

## Notes

- Domain language is in `CONTEXT.md`. The audience is the scattered ambitious person. Hyperactivity and attention deficit describe the texture of that mind. How common the pattern is now, including among neurodivergent people, is why the problem is urgent. A diagnosis is not a membership test.
- The buyer is the maker: a scattered ambitious person whose work is to ideate and then create or publish. See [Pay, keep paying, tell a peer](issues/06-pay-keep-tell.md).
- The spine is goal neglect: intentions already formed stop governing action under the load of many open projects. See [Name the spine](issues/04-name-the-spine.md).
- "What it looks like" means a day in the life plus a rough interface. Production visual design is outside this map.
- The business bar is: they pay, they keep paying, and they tell a peer. Venture-scale versus a smaller company is still fog.
- Skills: grilling and domain-modeling on decision tickets. Research tickets use the research skill. The interface ticket uses the prototype skill.
- Do not open tickets about whether the team can build it.
- Maintain [MVP scope](mvp.md) as a living index of settled commitments and unresolved boundaries. Engineering capacity is not a reason to reduce ambition. A directional feature does not enter the MVP through an agent assumption; settle its product behavior with the human first. The human added this rule on 2026-09-29.
- Tracker: local markdown in this directory. No hosted issue tracker is configured for the repo.
- This map decides. It does not build the product.

## Decisions so far

- [Why tools lose them](issues/02-why-tools-lose-them.md): they lose this person when upkeep outruns benefit as project load grows — visibility, integration, processing, and honest maintenance — more than from a lack of motivation.
- [What they already pay for](issues/03-what-they-pay-for.md): self-pay already spans free tiers and about $3–$12/month utilities, $17–$34/month planners, $19–$29/seat AI schedulers, and one-time buys around $10–$35 per Apple platform. Referral mechanics exist. Published retention rates were not found.
- [Load-bearing failure](issues/01-load-bearing-failure.md): sources do not crown one cause. The closest upstream failure is losing active control of intentions already formed, under concurrent load. Many projects and context switching amplify it. Organizing thoughts is mostly compensation. Inability to stay on one thing sits downstream.
- [Name the spine](issues/04-name-the-spine.md): the pitch is about goal neglect. Intentions stop governing action under the load of many open projects. Recall can survive. Switching and many projects amplify it. Organizing thoughts is compensation. Inability to stay on one thing is downstream.
- [One product or a series](issues/05-one-product-or-a-series.md): one product, two moments. Capture keeps parallel bursts and the person does not classify. Retrieval has to change the next act. The capture chrome stays directional. A second app for retrieval is outside this pitch.
- [When an intention comes back](issues/09-when-an-intention-comes-back.md): the intention governs action again at the moment of action, when a cue retrieves the intended act. Capture, storage, related notes beside the cursor, and a later review can all leave the person with notes they never act on.
- [Pay, keep paying, tell a peer](issues/06-pay-keep-tell.md): the first buyer is the maker. They stay because the dump and the creation session are one product. They tell another maker, and they tell from pride. The life on offer is passive productivity: calm upkeep, extreme making. The price band waits on the form.
- [Form of the creation session](issues/11-form-of-the-creation-session.md): the form is a point of decision. A dumped note appears inside an app they already have open. One click takes it where they chose. The piece comes after. There is no home. A rare label correction does not gate it. The list of apps and points of decision is Last pass on passive cues.
- [What capture must preserve](issues/10-what-capture-must-preserve.md): capture keeps separate thoughts, the maker's wording, and a source reference when available. Adjacent dialogs, a blurred overlay with a shortcut, and wikilinks to independent notes are in the MVP. Related notes during capture are optional. A multi-thought block gets a split hint whose acceptance is Explode, and a card shows a backend chunk. Capture is typing only. The standalone Explode hotkey and native speech are beyond the MVP.
- [Which price band](issues/12-which-price-band.md): the utility band, about $3 to $12 a month, with the exact price unset. The free tier is bring-your-own API keys, on the Obsidian model. Notes live on the maker's device by default. The paid tier adds cross-device sync and managed AI, so a paying maker needs no keys.

## Not yet specified

- The objects the product keeps — projects, intentions, captures, or something else.
- What the product does in the moments around the rough interface: a first session, an empty day, a week of abandonment, and whether it interrupts.
- Venture-scale versus a smaller company, once the price band is real.
- The name and the brand. The words "passive productivity" are a marketing stance, not yet a name.

## Out of scope

- Clinical treatment: diagnosis, therapy, medication, or any claim that the product treats ADHD.
- A general productivity suite for everyone.
- A buyer who is an employer, a school, a parent, or a clinician.
- Building the product on this map. The destination is the pitch and a settled MVP scope.
- Production visual design. The pitch stops at a rough interface.
