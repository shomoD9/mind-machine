# Scattered ambitious

The people this product is for: ambitious, many things open at once, and a mind that does not hold still.

## Language

**Scattered ambitious**:
A person with serious ambition who holds many open projects, switches context often, and whose intentions stop governing what they do. Hyperactivity and attention deficit describe the texture of that mind. The pattern is common now, including among people who are neurodivergent, and that prevalence is why the problem is urgent. A diagnosis is not required to be this person.
_Avoid_: ADHD user, modern mind, young person, neurodivergent (as the name of the audience)

**Intention**:
An aim this person has already formed. They can still recall it after it has stopped governing what they do.
_Avoid_: task, to-do, reminder

**Goal neglect**:
An intention that can still be recalled and has stopped governing action. Many open projects and context switching make it more likely. It is why planned and scheduled work fails this person, so it is the problem statement; spontaneity is the spine.
_Avoid_: forgetting, bad memory, lack of focus, distraction

**Spontaneity**:
Working from the thought that arrives, without schedules, timetables, or regimented routines. Some planning may remain; scheduled action does not. This is the philosophy the product serves and the spine of the pitch. The founder is the first user. The term is provisional until the research in `.scratch/scattered-ambitious-pitch/research/15-goals-without-planning.md` says whether a better name exists.
_Avoid_: impulsivity, lack of discipline, procrastination, chaos

**Ship**:
Put a piece out where other people can reach it: publish, release, post. The exit of an idea and the way a maker takes part in exchange with society. The product reads whether something shipped and never how it was received.
_Avoid_: launch metrics, engagement, finished (as a quality claim)

**Capture**:
A burst the person puts in by typing or speech, often several at once, without classifying it; separate thoughts and the maker's wording are preserved, allowing minor corrections for legibility. A capture retains a source or draft reference when available, and inferred connections remain revisable.
_Avoid_: note-taking session, inbox zero

**Dialog**:
A writing space for a thought, which can stay unfinished while the maker opens another beside it.
_Avoid_: chat (as the meaning of dialog)

**Wikilink**:
A deliberate link from a selected word or phrase in one dialog to another dialog. The linked dialog is an independent note: it can be retrieved and acted on by itself, and it keeps the selected phrase as its source reference.
_Avoid_: inferred connection (as a synonym)

**Explode**:
An action that divides a large captured block of text into constituent atomic notes by meaning. In the MVP it happens by accepting or adjusting a split hint. The standalone hotkey for a massive block is beyond the MVP.
_Avoid_: summarize, rewrite

**Retrieval**:
A dumped note offered back so the maker can take a point of decision. A note that only sits there has not retrieved anything. The product's second moment.
_Avoid_: browse, knowledge base, search (as the job), inspiration

**Act**:
A point of decision. One click takes a dumped note to a place the maker chose. The piece is made after that. A dump without a point of decision has not succeeded.
_Avoid_: engagement, review, streak, finished piece (as the act)

**Buyer**:
The maker. They use the product and they pay for it. An employer, school, parent, or clinician is not the buyer in this effort. Other scattered ambitious people are not the first buyer.
_Avoid_: account, customer, enterprise seat

**Maker**:
A scattered ambitious person whose work is to ideate and then create or publish. The first buyer.
_Avoid_: techbro, creative (as the whole audience), productivity user

**Dump**:
Thoughts a maker puts in when they arrive, as a stream, without organizing them. This is the point of thinking.
_Avoid_: inbox, second brain, system

**Point of thinking**:
The dump. A thought arrives, the maker puts it in, and does not organize it.
_Avoid_: ideation, brainstorm, point of making

**Point of decision**:
One click that takes a dumped note to a place the maker chose, so they can post it or build from it. The note is the card. This is the act. There are many points of decision. The note appears inside an app the maker already has open.
_Avoid_: landing, creation session, point of making, sitting down, inspiration

**Correction**:
A rare prompt. The maker confirms a suggested label on a note the product could not place. Not a point of decision, and not a home. Points of decision do not wait on it.
_Avoid_: organize, review, inbox zero

**Passive productivity**:
The maker does not organize and does not keep intentions in their head. The dump takes thoughts when they arrive. A point of decision brings one back, in a single click. What is passive is the upkeep, and the start. The piece is still made after the point of decision. The life being offered is calm and highly creative.
_Avoid_: passive income, doing nothing, automation (as the product)
